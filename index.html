01 · Brand Identity

Real Estate Identity

01

The brief

Non-negotiables
  • Hexagon Vastu
  • Rectangle Vastu
  • Upward arrow Vastu · growth
  • Reads as architecture My constraint
02

The process

Started with real façades. The hexagonal shading screen of Abu Dhabi’s Al Bahar Towers.

Built for precision. One mark that holds up on a visiting card or a three-metre sign.

Traced the shapes. Triangles in the façade became the hexagon and the arrow.

Fused, not stacked. Hexagon + upward arrow became one shape, so the Vastu brief reads as a single idea instead of three symbols.
